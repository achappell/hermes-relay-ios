Large-format whole-screen layout

Status: proposed; owner approval required. Nothing here is approved. Companion to LAYOUT.md (IOS-UX-F9 / ANDROID-UX-15, tag FB-LAYOUT). Twelve frames: iPad, Mac and Android tablet or foldable, each in voice view, typing view, reply with thinking text, and mute on. Recommended arrangement: one arrangement for voice and typing, the orb always small beside the transcript. Frames use fixed widths in CSS pixels, not device models; all text is neutral placeholder text. Mute (speaker glyph, label Mute/Unmute) and Interrupt (square stop glyph, label Interrupt) never share a glyph or label.

iPad

iPad: horizontal size class regular. Touch first; hardware keyboard shortcuts as in LAYOUT.md.

Mac

Mac: always large. Same arrangement as iPad; adds the Conversation menu-bar commands.

Android tablet / foldable

Android: WindowWidthSizeClass Expanded here; Medium frames collapse the sidebar (platform convention: list-detail shows one pane at Medium width).

Narrowing toward compact

Order: the sidebar collapses first, then the thinking pane becomes the compact thinking area above the composer, then the voice cluster folds into the compact controls.
