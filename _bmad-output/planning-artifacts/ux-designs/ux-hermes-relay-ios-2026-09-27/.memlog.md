- (event) Create run started, scoped to voice controls. Repo PRD-worktree activation skipped by user choice: no PRD exists here and AGENTS.md keeps product intent in the external hub.
- (decision) The main screen is for voice-focused talking; voice is the primary input there, typing secondary.
- (event) Observed (Jensen): repeatedly taps the big central mic orb expecting it to record her voice; it is decorative today.
- (event) Observed (Jensen, Amanda agrees): expects not to have to tap again to stop recording; speech should end on its own.
- (event) Observed (Amanda): rarely uses hands-free because it is too sensitive to interruptions (false barge-in while Hermes speaks); would like to use it.
- (decision) The central orb is the voice control: tap to talk, tap to send early, tap to interrupt. The orb glyph shows what a tap will do; the status line shows state then action (e.g. 'Ready · Tap to talk'). The bottom-bar mic button and 'Tap to record' label are removed (no duplicate control).
- (decision) A tapped recording ends on its own after ~1.5 s of quiet and sends (reuse the hands-free silence detector). Tapping the orb sends early; a Cancel link under the status line discards.
- (decision) Hands-free becomes 'conversation mode': after each reply it listens again automatically. Interrupting Hermes mid-reply is by tapping the orb. Voice barge-in is a separate opt-in (for headphones), off by default.
- (decision) The hands-free switch moves next to the orb, under its status line, so all voice controls sit together; the bottom bar is for typing (composer, prompt history, send).
- (event) Observed (Amanda): while Hermes is thinking, a lot of text streams in at the bottom (transcript area) and is unreadable.
- (decision) On the voice screen the transcript follows the voice: while Hermes is thinking, only the status shows (no streaming text); when it speaks, the reply text appears in step with the audio (word by word where speech timing exists, sentence by sentence otherwise). What you read is what you hear. The full text remains in history.
